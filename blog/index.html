    The Electromagnetic Theory of Light光的电磁理论

    The wave theory could already account for many details of interference, diffraction, and polarization, but what the ether that carries light vibrations actually was remained without a definite answer. In the early nineteenth century Thomas Young had conjectured that the ether transmitting electrical action and the ether carrying light vibrations might be one and the same, and he proposed an experiment on changing the refraction of a liquid by electrical action to test this conjecture. Michael Faraday also tried to influence the propagation of light by electricity and magnetism. In 1845 he found that a magnetic field can change the polarization direction of light passing through a transparent medium, providing experimental evidence for a connection between light and magnetism.波动理论已经能计算出许多干涉、衍射和偏振现象的细节，但承载光振动的以太究竟是什么，仍然没有明确的答案。托马斯·杨（Thomas Young）在19世纪初曾猜想，传递电作用与承载光振动的可能是同一种以太，并提出通过电作用改变液体折射的实验来检验这一猜想。迈克尔·法拉第（Michael Faraday）也尝试用电和磁影响光的传播。1845年，他发现，磁场能使穿过透明介质的光改变偏振方向，为光与磁之间的联系提供了实验证据。

    Early Theories of Light光的早期理论

    Humanity’s use of, and attempts to understand, light date back to prehistory. People made and improved optical devices while asking what light was and how the eye could see. Theory, experiment, practical experience, and technology became intertwined as these pursuits unfolded. As illustrated in Fig. 1(a), people were already making bronze mirrors some four thousand years ago, polishing metal surfaces to see their own reflections.人类利用和认识光的历史始于远古。人们一面制造和改进光学器物，一面追问光的性质以及眼睛为什么能够看见，理论、实验、经验和技术在这一过程中交错发展。如图1(a)所示，早在约四千年前，人们已经制作铜镜，用经过磨光的金属表面映照面容。古代留下的文字，也记录了对影子、倒像和水中物体的观察：今天我们知道，光在视网膜上成像，经感光细胞和大脑的处理形成视觉。古人却常把光的传播、眼睛的感知与心灵的认识放在一起讨论。一方面，有人认为物体不断发出“影像”，进入眼睛使人看见；相反地，也有人把视觉比作伸手触物，认为眼睛向外发出某种作用来感知物体。若眼睛能发光，为什么黑暗中仍看不见？柏拉图的解释为这种疑问留下了余地：眼内之火必须与外界日光结合，才能参与视觉。
